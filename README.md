  <h1>Xenith Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="meta">Effective date: June 25, 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Last updated: June 25, 2026</p>

  <p>Xenith ("the App") is developed and operated by Joseph Esses. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.</p>

  <h2>1. Data We Collect</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Health &amp; Fitness data</strong> — active energy burned, workout sessions, and sleep data read from Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission.</li>
    <li><strong>Food &amp; nutrition logs</strong> — meals, macros, and calorie entries you manually create or generate via AI scan.</li>
    <li><strong>Workout logs</strong> — exercises, sets, and reps you enter in the App.</li>
    <li><strong>Camera images</strong> — photos you take for AI food scanning. Images are sent to OpenAI's API for analysis and are not stored by Xenith.</li>
    <li><strong>Profile data</strong> — name, age, height, weight, fitness goal, and activity level you enter during onboarding.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. How We Use Your Data</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>To calculate personalized calorie and macro targets.</li>
    <li>To generate AI-powered food analysis and daily report cards.</li>
    <li>To sync and display your Apple Watch activity data.</li>
    <li>All data is stored locally on your device using iOS system storage (UserDefaults). We do not operate a backend server or database.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>3. Third-Party Services</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>OpenAI API</strong> — food scan images and text descriptions are sent to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI's privacy policy applies: <a href="https://openai.com/privacy" target="_blank">openai.com/privacy</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Open Food Facts</strong> — the App queries the Open Food Facts public database for nutritional information. No personal data is transmitted in these requests.</li>
    <li><strong>Apple StoreKit</strong> — subscription purchases are processed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Data Sharing</h2>
  <p>We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.</p>

  <h2>5. Data Retention &amp; Deletion</h2>
  <p>All data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes all associated data permanently. We have no copy of your data.</p>

  <h2>6. Children's Privacy</h2>
  <p>Xenith is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.</p>

  <h2>7. Changes to This Policy</h2>
  <p>Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.</p>
